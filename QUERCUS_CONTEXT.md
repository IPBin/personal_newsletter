- MGEA01 quizzes live on **Achieve (Macmillan)**, not in Quercus Assignments. Their due dates only appear in the instructor's weekly "Update" announcements.
- Todoist: everything is in **Inbox**, no projects/sections/labels. The user's own convention is short names, mostly priority p1. Tasks added on Nov 10 are prefixed with the course code.
