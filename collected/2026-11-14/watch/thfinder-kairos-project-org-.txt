Pathfinder is a selective fellowship for students organizing technical AI safety or AI policy university groups around the world. We provide mentorship, funding, and other resources to help fellows develop into on-campus leaders, preparing themselves and their group members for careers in the field. The Fellowship Funding Pathfinder organizers can apply for funding to support their group's activities, including everything from compute to speaker events to pizza. Graduate students can also apply for a stipend to support their time spent on the group. Mentorship We pair accepted fellows with experienced organizers who will support your planning, share tried-and-tested advice, and work closely with you on your specific goals and challenges. Community Our workshops, Slack community, and virtual coffee chats provide opportunities to connect with other fellows and share resources, ideas, support, and inspiration. Career support Many of our mentors have worked at top AI safety orgs like METR , RAND , and Anthropic . They're here to offer you advice and help you navigate your own successful future in the field. Recent Pathfinder participants have landed AI safety roles at orgs like Coefficient Giving , Constellation , UK AISI , and Redwood Research , and gone on to MATS , Astra , Generator , Horizon , and other top fellowships. Resources Pathfinder hosts workshops on important field-building skills like doing outreach on campus, involving graduate students, and facilitating in-person courses. We also share access to the AI Safety Groups Resource Center, which contains dozens of articles on how to build and manage an AIS university group. Impact Organizing is one of the highest-leverage things you can do for the field, and for your own trajectory. Pathfinder helps you help students at your university learn and upskill, so they can help the world safely navigate the arrival of transformative AI . While we usually don't select more than two fellows per group, we encourage everyone on your leadership team to apply. Please submit separate individual applications rather than applying as a group. Is the Pathfinder Fellowship paid? Graduate student fellows may receive a stipend to support their organizing time. Undergraduates are not eligible for this stipend. All Pathfinder fellows can receive funding for group expenses. How do we receive funding? For most groups, Pathfinder will arrange fiscal sponsorship, meaning a nonprofit will "adopt" you and provide a spending-capped credit card funded by Kairos. This helps avoid any tax complications of receiving the money personally, and provides you with an easy way to track your spending. Groups with existing legal entities will instead receive grants. Do I need a fiscal sponsor to apply? No. Pathfinder will arrange fiscal sponsorship for your group. Can I participate in Pathfinder more than once? Yes! If you've been through Pathfinder or its precursor program, FSP, before, you're welcome to apply in future rounds to receive additional funding and mentorship. What happens if my mentor isn&#39;t a good fit? We take great care in pairing you with the right mentor for your group, considering a variety of different factors. However, after the first three calls, you can express a preference to continue the program with a different mentor. How does Pathfinder relate to FSP and Coefficient Giving&#39;s university group funding? The Fieldbuilder Support Program (FSP) was a precursor program to Pathfinder, which now brings FSP's mentorship and Coefficient Giving's funding of AI safety groups under the same roof in order to better support organizers. Previous participants in either of these programs are welcome to apply to Pathfinder going forward. Pathfinder is funded by a grant from Coefficient Giving (formerly known as Open Philanthropy), but run by Kairos. Coefficient Giving is not involved with running the program or evaluating applicants to it. What happened to the Pathfinder Support Grants? Previously, the Pathfinder Support Grants provided funding without the mentorship aspects of Pathfinder. We now only fund groups with organizers in the Pathfinder program. If your group previously relied on PSG for funding, encourage your organizers to apply to Pathfinder. How can I refer someone? If you know someone who'd be a great fit for Pathfinder, send us a referral through this short form . We'll invite them to apply. Can I organize full-time? Yes. We're hiring Regional Organizers to work full-time and in person with the AI safety university groups in one city or region. See the Regional Organizer role to learn more and apply. Show more Who are we? The Pathfinder Fellowship is run by Kairos , a nonprofit whose mission is to help society navigate the transition to transformative AI . In addition to Pathfinder, we run several other programs: The Supervised Program for Alignment Research (SPAR) , which provides research opportunities for early-career students and professionals looking to enter the field. The Generator Residency , a 3-month residency where residents pitch, build, and ship projects that build capacity and infrastructure across the AI safety ecosystem. The Global Challenges Project , free three-day workshops on the risks from advanced AI and what to do about them. Questions? Contact us at pathfinder@kairos-project.org .
