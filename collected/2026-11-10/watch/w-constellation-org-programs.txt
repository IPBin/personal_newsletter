About Careers Contact us Programs Finding, connecting, and supporting top talent in AI safety Constellation’s programs provide time, resources, and structure to help researchers, policymakers, and practitioners make progress on the most critical questions in AI safety. Constellation's programs provide the time, space, and structure for researchers and strategists to make progress on critical problems. Learn More General Hosting &amp; Visitors Constellation hosts short-term visits and ongoing access for select individuals and teams working on AI safety. “I think this has probably been the period of four weeks in which I learned the most about any topic, throughout my whole life.” GABE WU, PROGRAM PARTICIPANT &amp; MEMBER OF TECHNICAL STAFF, OPENAI General Hosting &amp; Visitors Our Berkeley research center hosts select individuals and teams working on AI safety. We provide ongoing access to our Berkeley research center for established individuals and organizations in our network whose work aligns closely with our mission and benefits from proximity to our network. Constellation also offers short-term visits for AI safety and security talent – and for experienced professionals from other industries who are interested in contributing to the field.
