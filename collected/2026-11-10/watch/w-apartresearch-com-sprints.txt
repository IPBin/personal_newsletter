Who takes part You don’t need an AI safety title. You need skills that fit the question, time to work hard with a team, and the aim of producing something concrete. ML and AI safety researchers Hypotheses, evaluation designs, experiments and analysis. Engineers Experiment infrastructure, model integrations, datasets and tools. Domain experts Security, economics, finance, law, biology, psychology or governance. Researchers from other fields Methods AI safety hasn’t used well yet. Win a Sprint, get invited The top 5 to 20% of teams are invited to Apart Studio to turn their project into a research proposal. About 40% of Studio teams go on to the Apart Fellowship. No application form: your project is how you are selected. How our programs connect Sprint A weekend in a team on one AI safety question, online or at a local site. Stand out Apart Fellowship 6 to 16 weeks on your own project, with a research project manager, compute and publication support. Published research Papers at venues like NeurIPS, ICLR and ACL. No. Every Sprint is free to join, online and at local sites. Can I join without a team? Yes. Teams form at the start of the Sprint, in Discord and at local sites. What should a team submit? Something others can review: an experiment, evaluation, dataset, prototype, tool or analysis. Are Sprints online or in person? Most run online, often with in-person local sites, and a few are in person only. Each Sprint’s page lists its format and local sites. Can I host a local site? Yes. Use “Host a local site” on the Sprint’s page and we’ll list it. See the groups that have hosted local Sprint sites . How are prizes paid? By bank transfer, and team prizes are split equally between members. The submission and prize terms cover deadlines, tax and banking details, and the license your team gives Apart for its project. Can Apart support a hackathon we run? Yes. Propose a partner hackathon , or see the partner hackathons Apart has supported.
